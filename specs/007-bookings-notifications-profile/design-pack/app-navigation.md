## Layout and behaviour
- Compact tab bar (64 high + 34 home indicator, `surface`, top 1px `line`): 4 tabs for the booker: الرئيسية, المنشآت, حجوزاتي, حسابي. No notifications tab. The notifications entry point on Compact is the NotificationBell in the header of the four main screens (Home, Facilities, My bookings, Account), which pushes the Notifications screen with a back button.
- Guest: same 4 tabs, no bell, no notifications anywhere (NotificationBell component note and Account guest frames).
- Medium and up: AppNavigation rail (Medium, 88 wide, `surface`, 1px `line` divider, padding-top `space-4`, gap `space-3`) with 5 destinations in this order: الرئيسية, المنشآت, حجوزاتي, الإشعارات, حسابي. Items: indicator 56x32 radius-full over a 24 icon, label `micro`. Selected: indicator `primary-soft`, label `on-primary-soft`, filled icon (bell-fill for notifications). Unselected label `ink-muted`.
- Badge on الإشعارات: `danger` fill, `on-danger` `micro` text, min width 18, padding-x 5, radius-full, at the indicator's top corner (top 0, offset 6). Shows the unread count; hidden at 0. The Expanded sidebar (read, round 2) carries the badge in the الإشعارات row at the inline-end of the label; Large: same in screenshots.
- Trainer role (per component description): حصصي, مسح QR, الإشعارات, حسابي. Admin: 8 sections in rail/sidebar, 5 tabs in the tab bar. These variants exist for iOS only in Figma.
- Expanded sidebar (280): `surface`, 1px `line` divider, padding top `space-4`, padding x `space-3`, gap `space-1`; SprintLogo lockup on top (tile 32 + "سبرنت" Cairo 700 20/30, gap `space-3`, padding-bottom `space-3`); items 48 high (round 3, was 44; measured from the Expanded My bookings screenshot, pitch 52 = 48 + `space-1`; Brand/logo row stays about 44 and is not interactive), radius `radius-md`, padding x `space-3`, gap `space-3`, icon 24 at the start, label `label` (14/20 600); selected = `primary-soft` fill + `on-primary-soft` label + filled icon (bell-fill for الإشعارات, user-fill for حسابي, house-fill for الرئيسية); unselected `ink-muted`. Guest sidebar (Account guest): 4 items, no الإشعارات.
- Compact tab bar on pushed screens (round 2): the tab bar stays visible on pushed Notifications (الرئيسية selected, frame "pushed from Home") and on pushed Edit profile (حسابي selected). The selected tab on a pushed screen is the tab it was pushed from. Notifications pushed from Facilities, My bookings or Account: not drawn.
